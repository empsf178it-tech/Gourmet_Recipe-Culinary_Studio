METHODOLOGY

The Four Pillars of Studio Training

Our step-by-step framework takes you from fundamental food science to signature artistic creation.

01

Learn

Master ingredient mechanics, flour hydration formulas, butter fat percentages, and thermal baking science.

02

Practice

Develop muscle memory through hands-on dough folding, piping, temperature control, and oven deck management.

03

Refine

Perfect delicate finishes: mirror glazes, chocolate tempering, choux craquelin crunch, and gold leaf placement.

04

Create

Synthesize your skills to formulate original flavor profiles, seasonal tarts, and original dessert showpieces.
